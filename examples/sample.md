# Unit 1 — Introduction to Management Accounting

## Meaning

Management accounting is a modern concept of accounts as a tool of management. It is concerned with all such accounting information that is useful to management for planning, controlling and decision-making.

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India defines management accounting as a system of collection and presentation of relevant economic information relating to an enterprise for planning, controlling and decision-making.

## Characteristics

1. **Useful in decision making** — It assists management in choosing between alternative courses of action.
2. **Internal use** — The information is meant for management, not for external parties.
3. **Purely optional** — There is no statutory obligation to install management accounting.
4. **Future oriented** — It concentrates on what is likely to happen, though it may use past data for projections.

## Financial Accounting vs Management Accounting

| Basis | Financial Accounting | Management Accounting |
|---|---|---|
| Main users | External users such as investors and creditors | Internal management |
| Statutory requirement | Mandatory under company law and tax laws | Optional |
| Focus | Historical records and overall profit | Future planning, control and decisions |
| Statements | General purpose P&L and balance sheet | Special purpose reports for management |

# Chapter 2 — Standard Costing

## Material Variances

- Material Cost Variance = Standard Cost − Actual Cost
- Material Price Variance = Actual Quantity × (Standard Price − Actual Price)
- Material Usage Variance = Standard Price × (Standard Quantity − Actual Quantity)

### Example

| Material | Standard Qty. | Standard Rate | Actual Qty. | Actual Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 1,60,000 | 10 | 1,58,000 | 11 |
| B | 40,000 | 6 | 50,000 | 5 |
| C | 40,000 | 4 | 38,000 | 5 |
